## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not publisher statements:

- the per-item timing targets
- the "nine or more out of ten" practice target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists and study plans
- the prioritization and situational judgment answers, which follow common principles rather than any one agency's protocol
- all practice items, calls, maps and explanations, which are original
