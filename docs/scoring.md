# How CritiCall scoring works

Short version: the software scores you automatically, each module is scored in its own way, and the agency you are applying to decides what counts as a pass. There is no single national pass mark, and many agencies never show you your actual scores.

## Who scores the test

The publisher describes CritiCall as self-administering and self-scoring. The software scores almost every part of the test by itself as you go. No person marks your answers.

## How individual modules are scored

The publisher gives some detail for certain modules. Where it does not, this guide does not guess.

| Module | What the publisher says about scoring |
|---|---|
| Emergency-message decisions | No credit if you send the wrong agency, or if you do not respond within 15 seconds of the message appearing |
| Computerized Data Entry | Assesses speed and accuracy, and data must be in the right place on screen |
| Keyboarding | The score measures words per minute |
| Call Summarization 2 MT E911 | The score combines the accuracy of the data you enter with your answers to the multitasking and multiple-choice items |
| Practice items | Not included in your final score |
| Skipped questions | Penalized, unless the instructions for a section say otherwise |

Sources: [CritiCall Candidate Test Preparation Guide](https://criticall911.com/hubfs/Criticall-Website-Files/CritiCall_Candidate_Test_Preparation_Guide_2023.pdf) and the [CritiCall test list data sheet](https://criticall911.com/hubfs/Criticall-Website-Files/TestGenius-CritiCall-Test-List-and-Integrated-Resources.pdf).

Two practical rules follow. **Answer every question,** because skipping costs you. And **do not chase perfect speed at the cost of accuracy,** because modules like data entry count both.

## Who sets the pass mark

Each agency. The publisher does not publish a universal cut score, and agencies use results in different ways:

- **A minimum score.** The City of Colorado Springs says applicants must receive a score of 75 or better on the CritiCall test before moving on to its video interview. It may also require a separate typing test. ([source](https://coloradosprings.gov/node/101016))
- **A ranked list.** Arizona Department of Public Safety weights its CritiCall examination at 100% of phase one. Applicants who pass are ranked in score order on an eligibility list, and invited to the next phase in that order. ([source](https://azdps.gov/sites/default/files/2025-04/PCD_Exam_Plan_0425.pdf))
- **Pass to continue.** Grand Traverse County, Michigan, says a passing score must be achieved before a candidate is considered further. ([source](https://gtcountymi.gov/438/Emergency-Telecommunicator-Testing))
- **A required score with a chance to reapply.** Frederick County, Maryland, says that if you do not reach the required score, you are welcome to apply again. ([source](https://frederickcountymd.gov/8368/Hiring-Process))

You will see claims online that "most agencies" use one fixed percentage. Treat these as guesses. The only number that matters is the one in your agency's posting or invitation, and many agencies do not publish it at all.

## Will you see your score?

Often not. The TestGenius help center says scores belong to the agency or organization you tested for, so TestGenius cannot give them to you. It adds that most agencies do not give out results. They tell you whether you are moving to the next step, going onto an eligibility list, or did not pass. ([source](https://applicant.testgenius.com/knowledgebase/where-can-i-get-my-test-results))

If you want to know your score, ask the agency's hiring contact. Some will tell you. Many will not.

## What counts as a good score

Since you will rarely know the cut-off, aim to be comfortably above any plausible one in every module, not just on average. Two habits help:

- **Do not leave a weak module.** A ranked list rewards every extra point, and a minimum score can be lost to one bad module.
- **Score yourself honestly on practice.** If you are getting nine or more of every ten items right in the section guides, and staying calm under time pressure in a full timed run, you are in a good place. This is our study guidance, not a published standard.

## Retakes

The publisher does not publish a retake policy, because the agency controls testing. Rules vary. Frederick County, for example, invites candidates who miss the required score to apply again. The TestGenius help center says testing is hosted and scheduled by the hiring agency, so the agency is the only place to ask how soon you can test again.
