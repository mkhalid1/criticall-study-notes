# Test-day checklist

CritiCall is long, it uses audio, and it runs in your browser. Most test-day problems are technical or physical, not knowledge. This list handles those so your attention goes on the items.

## The day before

- [ ] **Find your invitation email** and note the deadline. Look for mail from onlinetesting@invitations.testgenius.com or info@governmentjobs.com, and check spam.
- [ ] **Use a laptop or desktop.** The help center says TestGenius works on Windows 10 or later and macOS 15 or later, and that some modules do not work on phones or tablets.
- [ ] **Pick a supported browser.** Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The help center says Brave, Opera and DuckDuckGo are not supported, and recommends Chrome or Firefox over Safari because Safari needs extra audio setup. Proctored tests need the latest Chrome.
- [ ] **Run a free sample test** on that computer and headset. The [TestGenius sample tests](https://criticall911.com/dispatcher-testing/applicants) exist mainly for this check.
- [ ] **Test your headset.** Wired headsets avoid Bluetooth dropouts.
- [ ] **Read the four decision rules once more.** Then stop studying.
- [ ] **Sleep.** Tired people swap digits.

## One hour before

- [ ] Quiet room, door closed, phone on silent and out of reach.
- [ ] Laptop plugged in to power.
- [ ] Close other programs and browser tabs. Turn off notifications.
- [ ] Turn Num Lock on if your keyboard has a number pad. The candidate guide says many test takers find it helps.
- [ ] Water on the desk. Use the bathroom.
- [ ] Block out at least three hours. The help center suggests two to three hours for one sitting.

## In the first minutes

- [ ] Read every instruction screen fully. Rules can change between sections, including whether skipping is penalized.
- [ ] Set the audio volume during the first audio item.
- [ ] Do the practice items at the start of each section. They do not count, and they show you the screen.

## During the test

- [ ] Use a single left click to select answers.
- [ ] Use Tab to move between fields.
- [ ] Answer every item. Skipping is penalized unless you are told otherwise.
- [ ] On emergency messages, decide within a few seconds and go straight back to your task.
- [ ] Between modules, sit back and take three slow breaths.

## If something goes wrong

- **Kicked out or the browser crashed:** close the test window completely and reopen your original test link. The help center says this should take you back to where you left off. ([source](https://applicant.testgenius.com/knowledgebase/kicked-out-of-the-test))
- **No audio in Safari:** follow the AutoPlay setup on your exam's landing page, or switch to Chrome or Firefox. ([source](https://applicant.testgenius.com/knowledgebase/what-browsers-are-))
- **Link expired or need more time:** only the agency can help. TestGenius support cannot extend or reopen a test. ([source](https://applicant.testgenius.com/knowledgebase/my-link-is-expired))

## Afterwards

- Do not expect your score. The help center says most agencies only tell you whether you are moving forward.
- Write down which modules felt hardest while you still remember. If you need to test again for another agency, that is your study list.

## Last practice before the real one

A short timed warm-up the day before, such as part of the [free CritiCall practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/criticall?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=zse9qc&utm_content=criticall), helps more than a long session on test day.
