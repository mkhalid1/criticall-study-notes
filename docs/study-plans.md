# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation has already arrived. Both start with the [5-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) so you know where you are weak. Expect about an hour a day on the 7-day plan, plus 15 minutes of typing practice.

If your job posting or invitation lists the modules you will take, cut any module that is not on the list and give the time to your weakest one.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read the [test overview](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Download and read the official [candidate preparation guide](https://criticall911.com/hubfs/Criticall-Website-Files/CritiCall_Candidate_Test_Preparation_Guide_2023.pdf). Note your three weakest parts. |
| 2 | Decisions and data entry | Read the [decision making](sections/decision-making-and-prioritization.md) and [data entry](sections/data-entry-and-keyboarding.md) guides. Make flashcards for the four decision rules and drill them until each answer takes under five seconds. Learn the field order. |
| 3 | Listening and memory | Read the [call summarization](sections/call-summarization.md) and [memory recall](sections/memory-recall.md) guides. Have someone read you phone numbers and short call scripts, and type what you hear. |
| 4 | Maps and lists | Read the [map reading](sections/map-reading-and-position-locating.md) and [cross-referencing](sections/cross-referencing-and-perceptual-ability.md) guides. Do five route descriptions out loud on any online map. |
| 5 | First timed run | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/criticall?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=criticall). Then read the [numerical](sections/probability-and-numerical-ability.md), [reading](sections/reading-comprehension-and-sentence-clarity.md) and [spelling](sections/spelling-and-proofreading.md) guides. |
| 6 | Fix the weakest module | Go back to the traps list for your weakest module. Redo its items without the answers. Read the [situational judgment](sections/situational-judgment-and-personality.md) guide. Try the free TestGenius sample tests to check your computer and headset. |
| 7 | Second run and rest | Take a second timed run in the morning. Compare with day 5. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop studying by early evening. |

Every day, add 15 minutes of typing practice: names, addresses and phone numbers, typed in capitals, with every character checked.

### How to review a missed item

The review is where the score comes from. For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Which rule or habit did I break?** For example: used real-world knowledge instead of the decision rules, swapped two digits, turned the wrong way.
2. **What should have stopped me?** Name the word, digit or sign.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "chunk every phone number into 3 and 4".

Keep these in one list. By day 6 the same two or three mistakes will keep showing up, and fixing those is worth more than any number of new items.

## 2-day plan

For when the test link has arrived and the deadline is close. Test links can expire, and the help center says only the agency can extend or reopen a test window, so check your deadline first. ([source](https://applicant.testgenius.com/knowledgebase/my-link-is-expired))

### Day 1: learn the rules

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 20 min | Read the official [candidate preparation guide](https://criticall911.com/hubfs/Criticall-Website-Files/CritiCall_Candidate_Test_Preparation_Guide_2023.pdf). Learn the four decision rules and the field order. |
| 3 | 40 min | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of every section guide, then do the practice items for your two weakest modules. |
| 4 | 30 min | Take a timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/criticall?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=criticall). |
| 5 | 20 min | Review every miss with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: set up and sit the test

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Run one of the free TestGenius sample tests on the computer and headset you will use. |
| 2 | 15 min | Redo yesterday's missed items, then say the four decision rules out loud. |
| 3 | 10 min | Read the [timing guide](timing.md) and the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). |
| 4 | Test | Sit the real test in a quiet room, with at least three hours free. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 2 and 4, run the sample test to check your device, and sit the test the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not look for "real" CritiCall questions.** The publisher keeps the software for agencies only, and your agency chooses its own modules. Learn the skills instead.
- **Do not test on a phone or tablet.** The help center says some modules do not work on them.
- **Do not cram the night before.** Tired people swap digits and miss signs, which is where most points go.
- **Do not skip typing practice** because it feels boring. It feeds several modules at once.
