# Memory recall

**In one line:** you see or hear a short set of details for a few moments, it disappears, and then you have to reproduce or recognize it.

The publisher describes several versions. In one, pairs of words appear on screen, vanish, and you are shown one word and asked for its partner. In the audio versions you hear strings such as serial codes, vehicle identification numbers or seven-digit phone numbers with no area code, and type them in from memory. The test checks memory and selective attention for both written and spoken information.

## What it measures

- **Short-term memory.** Holding a phone number or plate for the few seconds between hearing it and typing it.
- **Selective attention.** Noticing which details matter while the screen or the recording moves on.
- **Pairing.** Remembering which description went with which item, which is what you do when a caller describes three people and two cars.

## How the questions look

- **Word pairs.** A screen of pairs, such as a color and an object. After it disappears, you see one word and pick its partner.
- **Audio numbers.** A voice reads a code or phone number. When it stops, you type it.
- **Written details.** A short list of descriptions appears, then you answer questions about it.

The candidate preparation guide suggests a drill: have someone read you a seven-digit phone number, type it from memory as soon as they finish, and repeat with about five seconds between numbers until you are very accurate.

On this page the stimulus for each set is inside a collapsible block. **Open it, study it for the time shown, close it, then answer** without opening it again. A timer on your phone helps.

## A method that works

1. **Chunk.** Hold 5550349 as 555 and 0349, or even 555, 03, 49. Three chunks are easier than seven digits.
2. **Say it in your head.** Repeat the chunk silently until your fingers have typed it.
3. **Link pairs into a picture.** "Silver ladder" is easier to remember as an image of a silver ladder than as two separate words.
4. **Type immediately.** Memory for numbers fades fast. Do not wait for the whole recording to end if the format lets you type while listening.
5. **Do not second-guess** the first version you remember. Changing an answer after the stimulus is gone usually makes it worse.

## Traps that cost points

- **Reversing a pair of digits.** 0349 becomes 0394. This is the classic memory error.
- **Mixing partners.** You remember both words but attach them to the wrong pair.
- **Trying to memorise everything in order** when the question only asks for pairings. Work on the links, not the list.
- **Letting the previous item interfere.** When numbers come one after another, the last one bleeds into the next. Clear your head between items with a quick breath.

## Timing

Study times on the real test are short and set by the software. Train with times shorter than you think you need. Once 15 seconds feels comfortable for six pairs, drop to 10.

These 5 items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from CritiCall or any publisher's materials.

### Set A: word pairs

**Item 1.** Which word was paired with TRAILER?
A. Purple B. Brown C. Orange D. Silver

### Set B: phone numbers

<details>
<summary>Set B stimulus: open, study for 15 seconds, close</summary>

| Order | Number |
|---|---|
| First | 555-0418 |
| Second | 555-7362 |
| Third | 555-0927 |

</details>

**Item 2.** Which was the second number?
A. 555-7326 B. 555-7362 C. 555-3762 D. 555-0418

**Item 3.** Which of these numbers was in the set?
A. 555-0481 B. 555-0972 C. 555-0927 D. 555-7632

### Set C: vehicles

<details>
<summary>Set C stimulus: open, study for 20 seconds, close</summary>

| Vehicle | Color | Plate |
|---|---|---|
| Van | White | 3PLK208 |
| Sedan | Red | 8TWM551 |
| Motorcycle | Black | 6ZB4417 |

</details>

**Item 4.** Which plate belonged to the van?
A. 3PKL208 B. 3PLK208 C. 8TWM551 D. 3PLK280

**Item 5.** Which vehicle had the plate 6ZB4417?
A. Van B. Sedan C. Motorcycle D. None of them

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, items 1 to 5</summary>

**Item 1: A. Purple.** PURPLE went with TRAILER. *Why not B:* brown went with suitcase.

**Item 2: B. 555-7362.** *Why not A:* 7326 swaps the last two digits. *Why not D:* 0418 was the first number.

**Item 3: C. 555-0927.** This was the third number. *A* and *B* each swap two digits of a real number, and *D* swaps two digits of the second number.

**Item 4: B. 3PLK208.** *A* swaps L and K. *D* swaps the last two digits. *C* is the sedan's plate.

**Item 5: C. Motorcycle.**

</details>
